# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Engineers of all backgrounds and
experience levels are welcome. Critique designs and evidence, not people.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and deliberately disruptive behavior
are not acceptable in project issues, pull requests or discussions.

Give others room to explain their constraints. Acknowledge uncertainty, credit
prior work, and do not publish another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate content, close disruptive threads or
restrict participation. They should explain moderation decisions when it is
safe and useful to do so. Report ordinary conduct problems to the repository
maintainers; use GitHub's reporting tools for threats, abuse or private information
that should not be repeated in a public issue. Use any private maintainer contact
listed on the published repository when confidentiality is necessary.
